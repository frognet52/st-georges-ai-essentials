Module 1: Copilot, used safely

What the holder can do

Use the right tool

Works in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, signed in with a St George's account and covered by enterprise data protection, never a personal app.

Protect pupils

Never puts names, learning needs, medical or safeguarding details into AI. Describes the need, not the child, in line with GDPR.

Stay the professional

Checks every Copilot draft for accuracy, level and tone, and makes it their own before it reaches a class.

How it is earned

  1. Watch Module 1. A three-minute video covering three safe-use rules and three classroom wins.
  2. Pass the knowledge check. Three scenario questions, all answered correctly.
  3. Submit one real resource. A teaching resource made with Copilot Chat, with no pupil data, plus a one-line note on what was checked or changed.
